High Tides Community Tribute

Creative Development — High Tides Community Tribute

Using an in-game screenshot as a reference for the background, I roughly mark out the silhouettes to quickly figure out the best positions for each character, while keeping rough values in mind.

After settling on everything, I polish the line art of the background and make more detailed sketches for each character before finishing the line art using those sketches.

After that, I follow a similar process to the one I used for 9E, applying the base colours to each character before redoing the colouring with the background and sky lighting in mind.

9E Tribute

Creative Development — 9E Tribute

By first planning out traditionally sketched designs for each of my classmates with the help of photo references, I could then move on to sketching them all in the places they would be for the final piece, while keeping in mind where to place friendship groups.

After finishing that sketch in class, I scanned the paper and imported it into my program, Clip Studio Paint EX, to do another sketch and add the final details to make the line art more seamless.

After finishing the line art row by row, I moved on to colouring, first using the base colours that portray what the lighting would be like under white light, and then adding new colouring with the lighting of the sun and the reflection of the sky for the finished illustration.

Sentimental

My most recent animation! I made the audio along with the animation by using a 1990s text-to-speech voice that Microsoft had on older computers to do the voice of Senti, who is my own character I'd been developing for quite some time. Senti's design changed a lot while I developed the animation, with them being less humanoid before deciding on the lamp design.

She's Not There — The Zombies

This is my second most recent finished animation, using part of the song She's Not There by The Zombies – a band my grandad was in and whose music I enjoy. I used monochrome photography from archived Life magazine issues, as well as old photos of the band, as references for the lighting in this short.

You Are A Pirate!

Using the audio of a popular online pirate song I had heard, I used it to animate characters I created, inspired by my favourite game, High Tides. I focused on making each shot seamlessly follow the next, largely through the use of continuous or followed movement.
