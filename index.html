Sentimental

My most recent animation! I made the audio along with the animation by using a 1990s text-to-speech voice that Microsoft had on older computers to do the voice of Senti, who is my own character I'd been developing for quite some time. Senti's design changed a lot while I developed the animation, with them being less humanoid before deciding on the lamp design.

She's Not There — The Zombies

This is my second most recent finished animation, using part of the song She's Not There by The Zombies – a band my grandad was in and whose music I enjoy. I used monochrome photography from archived Life magazine issues, as well as old photos of the band, as references for the lighting in this short.

You Are A Pirate!

Using the audio of a popular online pirate song I had heard, I used it to animate characters I created, inspired by my favourite game, High Tides. I focused on making each shot seamlessly follow the next, largely through the use of continuous or followed movement.
